Information and consent

You are invited to take part in a research study on study spaces in the BUE library. The study looks at how the place where students sit relates to the way they work. If you agree, you will read a short text and answer questions about it (about 15 minutes), then answer a few short questions. Participation is voluntary, and you may stop at any time without giving a reason. Your answers are anonymous: we do not ask for your name or student number. The results will be used for academic research and may be presented at an academic conference or published. There are no foreseeable risks beyond those of answering the questions.

By continuing, you confirm that you have read and understood this information and agree to take part.

Reading task

Please read the instructions before you start.

  • You have 15 minutes for the text and the questions together.
  • Read the text, then answer the questions in order.
  • You can look back at the text at any time.
  • Work as quickly and as accurately as you can. If you are not sure, choose the answer you think is best and move on.
  • It is normal not to finish every question.
  • Please work on your own. Do not use other apps, notes or the internet.
  • When you have finished, press Finish at the end of the questions. If the 15 minutes run out, the task will end by itself.
